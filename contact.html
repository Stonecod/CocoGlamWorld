Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Choose a product on the Products page or select a service on the Services page, then submit your request through our booking form. We'll follow up quickly with confirmation and next steps.

How do I request a product?

On the checkout page, select "Product Request," choose the item you want, and submit your details. Our team will contact you with delivery and payment information.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery times vary depending on your location within Abuja. We'll provide an estimated delivery window when confirming your order.

Can I return or exchange products?

Yes, we offer returns and exchanges within 14 days of purchase for unused items in original packaging. Contact us directly to initiate a return.

Do you offer custom or bulk orders?

Yes! We offer customization for bulk orders and special requests. Contact us directly to discuss your requirements and pricing.

How can I book a spa service?

Click "Book Appointment" in the navigation, or visit our Services page and click "Book Appointment" on the service you want.
